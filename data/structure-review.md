## Decisions

### Structure

- "Support guides" -> using-wordpress, "Customization" -> editing-and-design (titles per §5 sketch).
- "FAQs" is renamed "Frequently asked questions" (slug faq).
- Administration screens mirror the wp-admin menu. Comments would hold a single page (comments-screen), so it lives in the Using WordPress > Comments topic section instead of its own screens group.
- Writing & publishing (25 articles in HelpHub) is split: core writing tasks stay at its top level; "Organizing your site" (post types, taxonomies, custom fields, permalinks, front page, search page, date format, SEO) and "Formatting & special characters" become children.
- "Comments" is a new Using WordPress section built from Publishing articles plus comments-screen.
- "Users, roles & profiles" is a new section (roles-and-capabilities, use-gravatars, reset-your-password, troubleshoot-login-issues). The three Users *screens* stay in Administration screens > Users.
- Block editor settings overviews get their own "Block settings" child under "Working with blocks" (depth 4) rather than one flat 20-item list.
- The Embeds blocks section uses the `embeds` article as its index; `embed-block` (the generic block) is listed first, then the provider embeds alphabetically.
- Block reference lists are alphabetical by title (not slug), with "(Deprecated)"/"(Removed)" pages last. Note verse-block sorts as "Poetry block".
- Site editor uses the `site-editor` article as its index, which removes the section/article slug clash.
- Default themes: 13 themes have both an article and a changelog. Twenty Fourteen and Twenty Thirteen have only a changelog (no theme article exists), so they get generated sections with a short intro holding just the changelog, keeping the newest-first sequence unbroken. There is no Twenty Eighteen theme.
